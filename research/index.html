Research

My work asks how to give learning systems structure they can trust: the shape of the data, the physics of the sensor and the relationships between modalities. It runs along three themes and is applied with partners in agriculture, food, health, energy and industry.

Topological deep learning

Topology describes what stays the same when data is stretched or corrupted: connected parts, loops and voids. I use persistent homology to turn anomaly maps into reliable pseudo-labels, optimal transport to compare persistence diagrams across scales, and hypergraphs and cell complexes to model relations between sensors and views in radar and fault diagnosis.

I co-wrote a review of topological deep learning, contributed to TopoX, and am teaching a tutorial on the topic at ACML 2026.

Hyperspectral and multimodal sensing

Depth from a single hyperspectral image, using chromatic aberration and defocus. IEEE TIP 2021

A hyperspectral camera records hundreds of narrow bands, so each pixel carries a spectrum rather than three colours. My PhD used these spectra for 3D vision, recovering depth from chromatic aberration and defocus. Since then I have worked on weak-signal unmixing, spectral features for food and crops, and on fusing spectral data with lidar, point clouds and radar.

Reliable AI under real conditions

Models trained in the lab meet new lighting, sensors and populations in deployment. I work on test-time adaptation that needs no source data or labels, on diagnosing out-of-distribution robustness from representation geometry, on training-free reasoning with vision–language models, and on learning when labels are scarce.

Projects

Chickpea quality from hyperspectral imaging

CSIRO, 2023 onward

Predicting protein and composition of chickpea varieties and flour from hyperspectral data, so breeders and processors can screen quality without wet chemistry.

  • Current Research in Food Science, 2025

Finding weak contamination signals in meat

CSIRO AI4M, 2022 onward

A benchmark for detecting faint E. coli signatures in steak with hyperspectral unmixing, comparing classical methods with transformer and CNN models. Follow-up work includes the WS-Net weak-signal unmixing model.

  • DICTA 2024
  • WS-Net (arXiv)

Cattle behaviour from video

CSIRO

Detecting and tracking individual cattle in the field and classifying their behaviour over time, for welfare, early disease detection and breeding. We released the CVB dataset and a CVAT-based tool that cuts annotation time by labelling only behaviour changes. Watch a clip.

  • CVB dataset (arXiv)
  • Animal action review, AI Review 2026

Sugarcane disease and resilience

Near-infrared hyperspectral imaging with CNNs to identify mosaic-resistant sugarcane varieties and to catch smut disease before symptoms show.

  • Mosaic resilience (arXiv)
  • Smut detection, IVCNZ 2021

Counting beneficial insects released by drone

YOLOv5 and DETR models count insects and larvae in mixtures dispersed by UAVs, so growers can check that natural pest control is spread where it is needed.
