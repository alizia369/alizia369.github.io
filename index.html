I build machine learning models that keep working when data is scarce, noisy or shifted.

Much of my work brings topology into deep learning: persistent homology, cell and simplicial complexes and hypergraphs give a network structure it can rely on when pixel-level cues fail. I pair this with hyperspectral and multimodal sensing, where I started, and apply it to crops and food, medical imaging, industrial inspection and radar.

I am a Research Fellow at La Trobe University with adjunct appointments at CSIRO and the Australian National University. I co-lead the CSIRO ML Workshop Series, supervise PhD and honours students, and received my PhD from Griffith University for work on 3D computer vision from hyperspectral images.

Research

Featured work

Topological deep learning

NeurIPS 2026

Topology-Aware Optimal Transport for Source-Free Test-Time Adaptation in Anomaly Segmentation

A. Zia, U. Ali, A. Khamis, M. U. Ramzan, A. Rehman, W. Xiang

TopoOT chains optimal transport across persistence diagrams from different thresholds and filtrations, then trains a light head online on the features that stay stable. Up to +24.1% mean F1 over the next best method on 2D benchmarks.

IEEE TPAMI 2026

Learning Topology-Aware Representations via Test-Time Adaptation for Anomaly Segmentation

A. Zia, U. Ali, A. Rehman, U. Ramzan, K. Han, M. Faheem, S. Qureshi, W. Xiang

TopoTTA replaces threshold heuristics with persistent homology on anomaly maps, producing pseudo-labels that keep defects connected. About 15% higher F1 on average across five 2D and 3D benchmarks.

NeurIPS 2026 workshop

Learnable Persistent Topology for Multi-View Radar Semantic Segmentation

NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Symmetry and Geometry in Neural Representations (NeurReps), Sydney

Learns cubical multifiltrations over a joint range–Doppler, range–angle and angle–Doppler representation and feeds persistent H0 and H1 structure into radar segmentation end to end. On CARRADA it matches the strongest published methods (63.85% RD and 44.70% RA mIoU) with the best car IoU in both views.

ECCV 2026 · HyperRadar

Learning Structurally Consistent Representations for Multi-view Radar Semantic Segmentation

A. Zia, M. U. Ramzan, A. Khamis, U. Ali, A. Rehman

HyperRadar learns hypergraphs over radar returns that belong to the same object and aligns range–angle, range–Doppler and angle–Doppler views with unbalanced optimal transport, so scattered echoes are segmented as one pedestrian, cyclist or car.

AI for health

IEEE JBHI 2026

Expert Fusion Network for Automated Blastocyst Morphology and IVF Decision Support

A. Zia, S. Qureshi, F. Ansarizadeh, M. Fozan, E. Kamawal, S. Anwer, C. Karmakar

EFN gives each embryo region graders use (trophectoderm, inner cell mass, blastocoel) its own expert subnetwork and fuses them with attention-gated weighting. Dice scores of 0.92, 0.91 and 0.90 on 2,344 annotated images, with cross-dataset validation.

PaperAll details
HICSS-60 (2027) Accepted

Context-Aware AI for Localised Pollen-Risk Guidance in Asthma Self-Management

Z. Sako, A. Zia, N. Wickramasinghe

A CLIP plant gate, YOLOv8 family detection, DINOv2 retrieval, allergen knowledge and live weather feed a rule-based risk score that a vision-language model explains. 91.2% top-1 accuracy on 26,978 plant images; the gate cut false acceptance of non-plant images from 83.8% to 0%.
