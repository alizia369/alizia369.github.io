How I teach

I try to build independent thinkers who can recover from being stuck. In lectures that means posing questions before giving answers and checking understanding as we go; in labs and projects it means real data, open-ended problems and plenty of peer discussion.

I mix formal lectures with project-based, collaborative and enquiry-based learning, and use online tools where they genuinely help: recorded lectures, discussion forums and short quizzes that show students where they stand.

With research students I start from their strengths, agree on a small first result, and widen from there.
